# Brand Foundation

## What We Are

We provide quick, affordable, and reliable at-home wellness sessions for everyday physical discomfort.

This is not a spa service.

This is not a luxury experience.

This is not a medical treatment.

We focus on helping people feel better through short, convenient wellness sessions delivered at home by trained and verified experts.

---

# Mission

Make everyday physical relief accessible, affordable, and available within minutes.

---

# Vision

To become the most trusted hyperlocal wellness network for quick physical relief.

---

# Brand Principles

### Fast

Customers should not wait hours or schedule days in advance.

Relief should be available when it is needed.

### Affordable

Wellness should not be expensive.

Short sessions at fair prices make relief accessible to more people.

### Reliable

Every session should follow a consistent process and quality standard.

### Safe

All experts must be trained, verified, and accountable.

### Respectful

Professional conduct is mandatory during every interaction.
